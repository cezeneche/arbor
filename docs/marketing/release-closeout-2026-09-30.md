The DPA page distinguishes its document date from the later sub-processor appendix date. The Privacy Policy, Terms and DPA visibly identify themselves as drafts. Unverified company details now appear as placeholders, not invented registration facts. The contradictory 90-day and 30-day post-closure promises were removed from the sample text; the actual schedule remains a decision for operations and counsel. Unverified sub-processor regions, transfer safeguards and model-provider data-use terms are explicitly pending confirmation.
- `legal-signoff-template.md` is the sample evidence and approval worksheet requested by the product owner. It records the entity, company number/address, ICO position, retention matrix, processor/transfer evidence and named approvers required to finalise the pages.

It did **not** verify the values, hosting region, encryption, sub-processor contracts, deletion/backup behaviour, or an external security assessment. A local `.env` points at a remote Supabase pooler, but that database was not modified.
- **Pending for sign-off:** authoritative company records, ICO position, actual retention and backup policy, verified provider/transfer and security evidence, and a qualified legal review of the finished legal documents. The sample is not approved contract language.
