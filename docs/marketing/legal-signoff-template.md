## Retention decision to make

Record the purpose, lawful basis, start event, active period, deletion trigger, backup expiry, legal hold exception, and actual deletion mechanism for each category: marketing enquiries, account/contact data, uploaded documents, extracted data, operational records, audit history, technical logs, and processor-held copies. Earlier drafts conflicted: the Privacy Policy said **90 days after account closure**, while the DPA promised **written deletion confirmation within 30 days**. Those unverified promises have been removed from the sample pages. A reviewer must settle the actual schedule and update both documents together. The [ICO storage-limitation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/) says retention periods must be justified by purpose, and its [controller–processor contracts guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/contracts/) covers return or deletion at contract end.

## Deployment and assurance evidence to collect

Attach the actual production hosting/database/storage regions, encryption configuration and provider terms; the current sub-processor agreements and transfer safeguards; backup/deletion procedure; access-review and incident process; and any completed external assessment report. The source code shows application controls, but cannot prove those operational facts. Record who checked each item, when, and against which environment.
